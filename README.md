# Modulus Business Suite

Modulus Business Suite is a modular SaaS operating system for Philippine SMEs.

The platform is designed around a multi-tenant architecture and provides integrated business modules including:

- Human Resources (HR)
- Payroll
- Timekeeping
- Leave Management
- Overtime Management
- Compliance Reporting

Future modules include:

- Finance & Accounting
- Procurement
- Inventory
- CRM
- Project Management
- Analytics

## Technology Stack

- Next.js 15
- TypeScript
- Bun
- Supabase
- PostgreSQL
- TailwindCSS
- shadcn/ui

## Architecture Principles

- Platform-first architecture
- Multi-tenant by design
- Engine-first development
- Pure-function business logic
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Row-level security (RLS)
- Audit-first compliance model
